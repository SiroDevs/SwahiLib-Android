SwahiLib is a Swahili dictionary and language reference app. Search words,
idioms, sayings, and proverbs, view details such as synonyms where available,
and keep using the app offline once content has synced.

Features:
* Search Swahili words, idioms, sayings, and proverbs
* View synonyms and details for each entry
* Offline-first: content is stored locally after the first sync
* Daily word and daily proverb highlights, with optional reminders
* Home screen widgets for the word/proverb of the day
